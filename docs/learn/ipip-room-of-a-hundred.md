# Room of a hundred

One decision. It applies to the IPIP-NEO-120 build only. It does not apply to the Understanding Society build.

The room-of-a-hundred picture from the mockup is kept as part of every answer. It takes the overlap percentage and visualizes it as a picture of a hundred people from each group in one room, showing how many from each group land in each part of the trait range, making the overlap feel concrete.

The score runs from 1 to 5. That range is split into five equal bands, each 0.8 wide, in this order: very low, low, average, high, very high. Very low is 1 up to 1.8. Low is 1.8 up to 2.6. Average is 2.6 up to 3.4. High is 3.4 up to 4.2. Very high is 4.2 through 5. A score on a shared edge counts in the higher band. A score of 1 is very low. A score of 5 is very high.

The human stated this decision on 31 Oct 2026 and asked that it be recorded for the IPIP-NEO-120 build. The five bands were set the same day.

Status: accepted. Five equal bands on the 1-to-5 score: very low 1–1.8, low 1.8–2.6, average 2.6–3.4, high 3.4–4.2, very high 4.2–5.
