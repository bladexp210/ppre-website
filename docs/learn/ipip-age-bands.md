# Age bands, no country filter

One decision. It applies to the IPIP-NEO-120 build only. It does not apply to the Understanding Society build.

Age bands, from the actual data range (age 10 to 99, with 106,855 rows under 18):

- 10–13
- 14–17
- 18–24
- 25–34
- 35–44
- 45–54
- 55–64
- 65 and above

These replace the six bands that started at 18. Those six had replaced the five mockup bands, which were only an illustration. Country remains dropped as a filter.

The small-sample warning applies to any thin band. See docs/index.md.

The comparison grid is 35 targets (30 facets plus 5 traits) times 2 sexes times these 8 age bands.

Group summaries: 35 × 2 × 8 = 560.
Pair comparisons, two sex-and-age groups on the same target: 16 × 15 / 2 = 120 pairs per target, times 35 = 4,200.
