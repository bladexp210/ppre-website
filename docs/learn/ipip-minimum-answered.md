# Minimum answered items

One decision. It applies to the IPIP-NEO-120 build only. It does not apply to the Understanding Society build.

Require at least 2 answered items, meaning non-zero values, before showing a facet score or a trait score. If fewer than 2 items are answered, do not show a score. Treat that as no answer for that comparison.

This applies to all 30 facets and all 5 traits. Each facet has 4 items. Each trait is the 24 items of its six facets.
