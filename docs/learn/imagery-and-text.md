# Imagery and text

One design rule. It applies to both builds:

- the IPIP-NEO-120 build
- the Understanding Society build

Mix imagery and text in a way that makes understanding clear and easy, makes the ideas stick, and replaces the earlier misconceptions people could have held. A picture is worth a thousand words, so visuals help.
